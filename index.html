In short

Nine points from the correspondence

  1. Rooms were marketed with a private bathroom. During the works that bathroom was stripped and unusable for weeks.
  2. On 24 July 2026 NextGen Support promised individual notice with exact dates and solutions. The 6 August schedule set the bathroom work for 13 and 14 August. It was not finished in two days.
  3. The information was short and kept changing: I had packed for one night, I had been told the work would finish by 17:00, then Sorell Hotel Rex was “until Monday”, and on return the bathroom was still unfinished.
  4. The 24 July email announced construction and hazardous-material remediation for the building, in those words, from August until the end of September.
  5. Through 2026 the company repeatedly required access to the room at short notice.
  6. After the detailed written complaints, Melanie Kumpusch’s only reply on the urgent thread, on 24 August 2026 at 11:45, was: “Thanks for your message. Please get in touch directly with Alexander Olsson.”
  7. I paid the full rent while I was displaced and while the room was not in the condition that had been promised.
  8. A tenants’ association sent a registered letter on 3 September 2026, covering a rent reduction, the CHF 206, and damages. In the correspondence through late September, it is unanswered.
  9. Some urgent emails to listed staff addresses bounced. Two of those addresses were invalid.

Der Untermietvertrag lief vom 1. Dezember 2025 bis zum 30. September 2026. Ich habe die volle Miete weitergezahlt, während ich auswärts war und das Zimmer nicht dem zugesagten Zustand entsprach. Die CHF 150 vom 21. Juli und die Entsorgungskosten bis CHF 1,000 vom 24. September stehen im Verlauf als Ankündigungen, nicht als Beträge, die ich hier als bezahlt aufführe.

  • CHF 1,450 Monatliche Miete im Untermietvertrag CHF 1,350 Miete plus CHF 100 Nebenkosten.
  • EUR 1,384–1,628 Parallele Airbnb-Belastungen pro Monat Neben dem Untermietvertrag, ungefähr in dieser Spanne.
  • CHF 1,527 Airbnb, Dezember 2025, ungefähr Am 9. Dezember stellte ich diesen Betrag den vorgeschlagenen CHF 1,450 gegenüber und fragte, ob der Dezember doppelt belastet würde. Die Antwort auf diese Frage steht nicht in diesem Bericht. Ich bat ausserdem, die übrigen Airbnb-Monate ab Januar zu stornieren.
  • CHF 206 Von mir bezahlt, um den 1. September 2026 Damit mein Partner duschen konnte. Bis mittags gab es kein Wasser.

EUR 4,900 habe ich später im Resolution Center von Airbnb beantragt. Der Antrag beschrieb den Auszug am 13. August, zu dem ich am Morgen verpflichtet war, die Ansage von ein bis zwei Tagen und fast drei Wochen Hotels.

Am 29. September 2026 kürzte Airbnb den Aufenthalt um 37 Nächte und zahlte zwei Beträge: EUR 981.93 für ungenutzte Nächte und EUR 835.29 als Lösung des Kundensupports. Zusammen EUR 1,817.22. Das sind nicht die beantragten EUR 4,900.

Amounts in the correspondence

Rent while the room was uninhabitable

The sublease ran from 1 December 2025 to 30 September 2026. I went on paying the full rent while I was staying elsewhere and while the room was not in the condition that had been promised. The CHF 150 of 21 July and the disposal costs of up to CHF 1,000 on 24 September appear in the record as warnings, not as sums I am listing here as paid.

  • CHF 1,450 Monthly rent under the sublease CHF 1,350 rent plus CHF 100 Nebenkosten.
  • EUR 1,384–1,628 Parallel Airbnb charges each month Alongside the sublease, about this range.
  • CHF 1,527 Airbnb, December 2025, about On 9 December I set this figure against the proposed CHF 1,450 and asked whether December would be charged twice. The answer to that question is not in this account. I also asked to cancel the remaining Airbnb months from January.
  • CHF 206 Paid by me, around 1 September 2026 So my partner could shower. There was no water until midday.

I later requested EUR 4,900 through Airbnb’s Resolution Center. The request described being required to leave on the morning of 13 August, the promise of one to two days, and almost three weeks of hotels.

On 29 September 2026 Airbnb shortened the stay by 37 nights and paid two amounts: EUR 981.93 for unused nights and EUR 835.29 as a customer-support resolution. Together, EUR 1,817.22. That is not the EUR 4,900 I requested.
