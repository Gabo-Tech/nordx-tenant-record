NextGen · NordX · November 2025 – September 2026

NordX from November 2025

Around 19–20 November 2025 I moved, through Airbnb, into a room at NordX – The NEXT Student Home, Nordstrasse 70, 8006 Zürich. NextGen Properties GmbH was the landlord. An Untermietvertrag then ran from 1 December 2025 to 30 September 2026. When we moved in, the room and the common areas were clean, tidy, and in good condition. This record starts there.

From the first weeks there were two payment paths: CHF 1,350 plus CHF 100 Nebenkosten, CHF 1,450 in all, and parallel Airbnb charges of about EUR 1,384 to EUR 1,628 a month. On 9 December 2025 I was asked to sign an “Initial Rent Form”. Airbnb had already charged December, about CHF 1,527. I asked in writing whether that month would be charged twice, and I asked to cancel the remaining Airbnb months from January. The written record on this page does not contain an answer.

After a couple of weeks, new people moved in. The common areas stayed dirty, broken things stayed broken until August, and I saw no effective maintenance. From February through July 2026 the company required short-notice access, mostly for window measurements. In my account, staff also arrived without prior notice, about once or twice a month. None of that changed anything. On 19 August Melanie Kumpusch wrote that window replacement was postponed to 1 October 2026, after the contract ended on 30 September. The 6 August plan set the bathroom work in our room for 13 and 14 August. I was told one to two days. It lasted almost three weeks: hotels, an unfinished bathroom, full rent, an unanswered registered letter, a late offer of a replacement room, and key return on 30 September between 09:00 and 10:00. On 29 September Airbnb paid EUR 1,817.22. I had requested EUR 4,900.

Written by an anonymous former tenant. One account of NextGen, NextGeneration, NordX, and Nord X, not a pooled score. Compiled on 6 November 2026. The tenancy record ends in late September 2026.

Meine Schilderung · 6. November 2026

Was ich bei NordX gesehen habe, bevor die Sanierung begann

Was ich gesehen habe, in der Ich-Form. Beim Einzug

Als wir um Mitte bis Ende November 2025 einzogen, waren das Zimmer und die Gemeinschaftsräume sauber, ordentlich und in gutem Zustand.

Nach ein paar Wochen

Nach ein paar Wochen zogen neue Leute ein. Die Gemeinschaftsräume wurden danach dauerhaft schmutzig. Ich sah nicht, warum ich eine zusätzliche Reinigungsgebühr zahlen sollte. Ich hielt meine eigenen Sachen sauber und musste die schmutzigen Gemeinschaftsräume trotzdem benutzen.

Besuche im Zimmer

Keine dieser Kontrollen und keines der Aufmasse hat vor August 2026 etwas sichtbar verbessert. Was geprüft werden sollte, damit man es repariert oder verbessert, wurde nicht verbessert. Im August begann die Sanierung. Der weitere Verlauf mit Datum steht unten.

My account · 6 November 2026

What I saw at NordX before the renovation

What I saw, in the first person. My recollection of daily life at NordX, NextGen Properties, and The NEXT Student Home, Nordstrasse 70, also searched as NextGeneration, Next Generation, and Nord X. Written down on 6 November 2026. Kept apart from the dated company emails. Those emails are in the next section and in the chronology.

When we moved in

When we moved in, around the middle to the end of November 2025, the room and the common areas were clean, tidy, and in good condition.

After a couple of weeks

After a couple of weeks, new people moved in. The common areas then became persistently dirty. The shared kitchen and other shared spaces were left full of trash and leftover food. Food sat on the counter for more than two weeks. It rotted, with flies and other insects.

There were no shared cleaning utensils for those areas, and no shared cleaning room or cupboard that tenants could use to clean them.

I would have cleaned the shared kitchen myself. I refused to cook in those conditions. I did not have the materials, or the time, to clean for everyone else.

What stayed broken

Broken things stayed broken until the later renovation. That included doors that had come off or fallen, and other damaged fittings. Some of it was left sealed. Some of it was left as it was. There was no effective maintenance in this period. Before August 2026, nothing that was broken was actually fixed or improved.

The fee and the weekly cleaning

Over the following months the conditions got worse. Management threatened extra charges for cleaning. That matches the hygiene warning of 21 July 2026, which threatened CHF 150 per resident for extra cleaning. They then arranged for a person to clean about once a week. In my account that cleaning was only superficial and did almost nothing to the common areas.

I did not see why I should pay an extra cleaning fee. I kept my own things clean and still had to use shared spaces left dirty.

Visits to the room

Besides the emails that announced access at short notice, staff also appeared without prior notice, about once or twice a month. They entered the room and moved our belongings even when they had said they were only checking a specific outlet, a window, or something similar. It felt like an unannounced inspection.

None of those inspections, and none of the measurements, led to a visible improvement before August 2026. Things that were supposed to be checked so they could be fixed or improved were not improved. In August the renovations began. The rest of the story, with dates, is below.
